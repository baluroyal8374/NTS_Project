Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

Narayanadri Technology Solutions (“NTS”, “we”) runs this website and training centres in Bengaluru, Tirupati and Nellore. This page explains what personal data we collect through the website and how we use it, in line with India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

What we collect

Enquiry form: your name, phone number, email address, the course and branch you choose, and any message you write. Visit counter: we count visits per day (one per browser per day). The counter stores a date and a number only, with no name or contact details. Your browser keeps a small flag so you are counted once a day. Portal login: if you are a registered student, intern or employee, your account details are used to run your portal account.

Why we use it

We use enquiry details to reply to you, tell you about courses, batches and fees, and keep a record of your enquiry. We do not sell your data.

Who can see it

Enquiries are received through a Google Form and are visible to NTS staff who handle admissions. Google processes the form data for us. We share data with others only where the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details for as long as needed to respond to you and manage admissions, then delete them or make them anonymous.

Your choices

You may ask us to access, correct or delete your personal data, or withdraw your consent to be contacted, by emailing hr@narayanadritech.com or calling +91 78929 28830. If you are not satisfied with our response, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India once it is operational.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
